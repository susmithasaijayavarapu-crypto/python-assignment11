1   Static vs. Interactive Visualizations

Static visualizations such as traditional PNG images, PDFs, or printed charts present a fixed snapshot of data.
They are ideal for quick publication in printed reports and ensuring consistent visual formatting across all
platforms. However, they limit the user to a single pre-determined view.

Interactive visualizations allow users to actively manipulate the display through zooming, panning, hovering over data points
for detailed tooltips, and filtering parameters dynamically. 


2  Advantages of Dashboards for real time data exploration
Dashboards combine live information into one easy to read screen. Instead of waiting for
weekly reports or searching through database files, managers can see important business numbers update in real time.
This makes it easy to spot sudden problems, track progress, and make fast, smart decisions right as things happen.

3  Improving Data Communication with Plotly
Interactive tools like Plotly bridge the gap between complex analytical datasets and non-technical 
stakeholders in professional settings
Web-based features like hover cards display precise values only on demand, 
keeping main charts clean, modern, and uncluttered.

Cross-filtering, legend toggles, and range sliders allow stakeholders to explore scenarios relevant to their specific
department without needing a data analyst to generate new custom reports.

Exporting interactive visual artifacts directly to standalone HTML files or embedding them into web platforms 
ensures rich analytical reports can be shared easily across teams using standard browsers.
